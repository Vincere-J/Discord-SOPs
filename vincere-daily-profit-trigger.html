Setting a Daily Profit Trigger for Consistency Rules

A consistency rule caps how much of your total profit target can come from a single day, so no one trading day accounts for more than a set percentage of the overall payout. A daily profit trigger enforces that automatically: it locks your account once you hit the dollar amount tied to that percentage, keeping you compliant instead of grinding past it on a green day.

  1. 1

    Work out your target dollar amount

    1 min

    This trigger is only for accounts that have a consistency rule, not a max drawdown rule. Setting one on a max drawdown account limits how much room the algos have to work with, so only use this when a consistency rule is what you are protecting against.

    1. Find your account's total profit target and its consistency percentage (example shown: a 50k account aiming for $4,100 with a 50% consistency rule, which is the most common rule you will see).
    2. Take that percentage of the target. For a 50% rule on $4,100 that is about $2,050.
    3. That number is your daily profit trigger amount for this account.

    Direct to Funded example

    20% consistency rule on a $9k target

    Direct to Funded accounts carry a 20% consistency rule. On a $9,000 profit target, 20% works out to $1,800, so the Daily Profit Trigger on those accounts should be set to $1,800.

    Every prop firm sets its own consistency percentage; confirm your own account's profit target and consistency percentage before setting the number. This step only applies to accounts with a consistency rule, never to max drawdown accounts.

  2. 2

    Open Risk Settings in Tradovate

    1 min

    Risk levels for each account live under Risk Settings, not on the main trading screen.

    1. Go to trader.tradovate.com and log in with the Tradovate credentials for the prop firm account you are setting this up on.
    2. Click the blue shield icon (Risk Management) in the icon column on the right side of the screen.
    3. Choose Risk Settings.
    0:28
  3. 3

    Set the Daily Profit Trigger for the account

    1 min per account

    This is the setting that actually closes out your trades once your P&L reaches the amount you calculated.

    1. Under the account, find Daily Profit Trigger in the list of risk levels.
    2. Turn it on.
    3. Enter the dollar amount you calculated in step one.
    4. Push Save.
    What happens next: if that account's P&L reaches the amount you entered, Tradovate closes all trades for that account for the rest of the day.
    0:32
  4. 4

    Repeat for every account

    1 min per account

    The trigger is set per account, so an account without it configured has no protection at all.

    1. Go back to the Accounts list.
    2. Open Risk Settings for the next account.
    3. Set its Daily Profit Trigger the same way and save.
    4. Confirm you see the update-successful confirmation for each account.
    0:48

  5. Your accounts are protected

    Every consistency-rule account now has a Daily Profit Trigger set to its consistency percentage of the profit target. On a green day, Tradovate will close out that account's trades once it hits the number you entered, keeping you inside your consistency rule automatically.
