Resetting Your Vincere License Key

A license key reset comes up in a few situations, an account not taking trades, a prompt from the updater, or when hooking up the Vincere Ninja Manager. This walks you through resetting the key from your whop.

0 of 5 steps complete

Tick each step as you go.

  1. 1

    Know when to reset your license key

    Before you start

    A license key reset comes up for a few different reasons, not only a stuck account. Resetting is the safe action here, it keeps your key the same but clears whatever is stuck.

    1. Your account is not taking any trades
    2. You are running the updater and it prompts you to reset the key
    3. You are hooking up the Vincere Ninja Manager
    Do not push Shuffle unless you have been told to. Shuffle changes the key itself, and you would then have to update the key in every strategy it is integrated with.
  2. 2

    Open your license key page

    In your whop

    The license key lives inside your whop, under the License Keys and Algorithm Files section.

    1. Go into your whop
    2. Scroll down to License Key in the left sidebar
    3. Open the License Key page
  3. 3

    Open the three dots menu next to your key

    License Key page

    The reset action sits behind the three dots button next to your license key field, not on the key field itself.

    1. Find Your License Keys at the top of the page
    2. Click the three dots button to the right of the license key field
  4. 4

    Click Reset

    Three dots menu

    Reset clears the stuck state without changing the key value, so nothing else needs to be updated afterward.

    1. Click Reset from the menu
    2. Leave Shuffle alone unless you were specifically asked to use it
  5. 5

    Reconnect in NinjaTrader, if that is why you reset

    Only when the reset was for a stuck account

    NinjaTrader needs a clean restart so it picks up the reset key cleanly across all your accounts. This step is only needed when the reset was to fix an account that was not taking trades.

    1. Close all open trades
    2. Disconnect everything in NinjaTrader
    3. Restart NinjaTrader
    4. Reconnect all of your accounts
    If you reset the key because the updater prompted you, or while hooking up the Vincere Ninja Manager, this restart sequence is not needed, just continue with what you were doing.

  6. You are reconnected

    Your license key has been reset and NinjaTrader is back online across all your accounts. Give it a few minutes and confirm trades are being taken again.
