Fixing delayed data in NinjaTrader

A "Real time market data required" banner or charts running 10 minutes behind almost always traces back to one prop firm connection missing its live-data agreement, not a chart setting. Work through these checks in order to isolate and clear it.

Cash account? Delayed data there is a paid market-data subscription issue, not a prop firm agreement. See the NinjaTrader market data setup guide instead.

0 of 7 steps complete

Tick each step as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

  1. 1

    Confirm the symptom

    Delayed data shows up as a specific banner or a chart that will not move, and confirming exactly what you are seeing rules out a chart setting before you touch any connection.

    1. Look for a Real time market data required banner, or a message that data is delayed by 10 minutes.
    2. Check whether the chart itself is frozen or moving on a visible delay instead of live.
    3. This is usually not a chart setting. It points to a connection or agreement issue underneath.
  2. 2

    Isolate the connection causing the delay

    One prop firm connection not having live data fully active can poison the whole feed, so isolating it one firm at a time finds the exact culprit instead of guessing.

    1. In NinjaTrader, go to Connections and choose Disconnect All.
    2. Reconnect one prop connection at a time.
    3. After each reconnect, watch the chart: does it move live, or does the delayed-data label appear again?
    4. The firm that brings the delayed label back is the one that needs the fix below.
  3. 3

    Check for orphan sim accounts

    A leftover simulation account with no prop firm attached can also trigger the delayed-data banner, even though it is not the account you are trading.

    1. Look through your connected accounts for any sim account that has no prop firm login behind it.
    2. Disconnect any orphan sim account you find.
    3. Recheck the chart after removing it.
  4. 4

    Rule out a prop firm you have no account with

    Delayed data does not only come from the firms you actively trade. A prop firm connection left in NinjaTrader for a firm you do not hold an active account with can trigger the same delayed-data banner. Clearing these before you touch any agreement stops you from chasing the fix on the wrong connection.

    1. Look through your connections for any prop firm tied to an account you no longer, or never did, hold.
    2. Check that firm’s own portal to confirm whether the account is actually active and funded.
    3. Disconnect or remove any connection for a firm you do not have an active account with.
    4. Whatever live prop firm remains is the one you actually trade, and the connection you will fix in the steps below.
  5. 5

    Find the Tradovate credentials for the affected firm

    Once you know which firm is causing the delay, its Tradovate username and password are what let you sign into the account that controls the live-data agreement.

    1. Log into that prop firm's dashboard.
    2. Find the Tradovate credentials for that account. The location differs per firm, but it is usually next to the account or under a Show Credentials button.
    3. Keep the username and password handy for the next step.
    Keep a document with each firm's Tradovate username and password together so you can move quickly if this happens again.
  6. 6

    Sign the non-professional market data agreement

    Signing this agreement is what activates live data for that connection. A missing or incomplete agreement is the most common cause of the delayed-data banner.

    1. Go to trader.tradovate.com and sign in with the credentials from the step above.
    2. Complete your profile if prompted.
    3. On the market data agreement, choose Non-Professional, then check both I agree boxes and sign your name exactly as written on the form.
    4. In Application Settings, confirm the Subscription section shows market data active for that account.
    Non-Professional just means you are not managing someone else's money, not part of a board or exchange, and do not hold a Series 7 or similar license.
  7. 7

    Reconnect in NinjaTrader and confirm live data

    The agreement only takes effect once NinjaTrader is restarted and the firm is reconnected, so this step closes the loop and confirms the fix actually worked.

    1. Restart NinjaTrader.
    2. Reconnect the firm you just fixed.
    3. If NinjaTrader prompts you to roll the contract forward, always select Yes.
    4. Confirm the delayed-data label is gone and the chart is moving live.
    Still delayed after reconnecting? If the agreement is signed and the contract is rolled forward but the feed still will not go live, the connection is almost certainly a firm you do not hold an active account with. Go back to step 4 and confirm the account is active in that firm’s own portal.

  8. Live data restored

    The affected connection is reconnected with its market data agreement signed, and the delayed-data label should be gone. Keep your Tradovate credentials document handy in case another firm needs the same fix later.

Common root causes

Every delayed-data case traces back to one of these.

Tradovate non-professional agreement not signed

The most common cause. See step 5 and step 6 above to find the credentials and sign the agreement.

Prop firm account not active under that login

Confirm the account is active in the prop firm's own portal before troubleshooting further on the NinjaTrader side.

Wrong or limited connection selected

A Vincere Sim or other limited data connection selected instead of the live prop connection will always show as delayed.

One firm connection poisoning the whole feed

See step 2 above. Disconnect All, then reconnect one firm at a time to isolate which one is responsible.

Orphan sim account with no prop firm attached

See step 3 above. Disconnect any sim account that has no prop firm login behind it.

Cash accounts

Cash account delayed data is a paid market-data subscription, not the prop firm non-professional agreement. Use the NinjaTrader market data setup guide to purchase and activate the subscription instead of working the steps above.
