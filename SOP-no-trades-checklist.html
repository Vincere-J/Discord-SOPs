My algos are not taking trades

Before you reach out, take this checklist one step at a time, top to bottom. Most no-trades cases come down to one simple layer that is not green yet: connection, live data, strategy state, contract, account, updater, or license. The fix is usually quick once you find the layer. This checklist works for both prop and cash accounts.

⚠️ Vincere Ninja Manager users only

Step 0. Do you use the Vincere Ninja Manager?

If you do not use the Ninja Manager, skip this box and start at check 1. If you do, tap to open and run these quick checks first.

⚠️ Read this first. Everything in Step 0 applies only if you run the Vincere Ninja Manager. If you connect NinjaTrader yourself by hand, this whole box does not apply to you. Close it and start at check 1.

Run these quick checks first, because the manager controls your connections and which strategies get enabled each morning. The Ninja Manager is an early version, so treat it as a helpful assistant, not the final source of truth: verify things yourself each morning.

  1. Confirm the Ninja Manager is actually running on the VPS: look for its window, or its icon in the Windows taskbar / system tray (the little arrow at the bottom-right of the screen: click it to expand the tray). A lot of people miss it because it is tucked away in there. If it never launched, open it again. On the blue Windows SmartScreen box, click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Open the manager and check today's log. You should see this morning's full sequence: disable, disconnect, reconnect, enable. If nothing ran, open Settings, then General, and confirm the Morning Startup Time is set to 6:00 AM or later. It runs on Central (VPS) time, not your home time zone.
  3. In Settings, open the Connections tab, find the row for your connection name, and confirm Connect on Startup is ticked for every live account you expect to trade. Anything left unticked is left completely alone, so that account never reconnects.
  4. Prop firms: in that same Settings → Connections tab, review the connection and confirm the cycling mode matches your account type. Prop firm accounts cycle by period: Period 1 enables your 0-dash and 1-dash strategies, Period 2 enables 0-dash and 2-dash. A strategy that is off in the current period is expected, not broken. Cash accounts should use the simple enable all strategies mode instead. The Cycle Status panel shows your mode, current period, and the next switch date.
    Prop firms: make sure cycling is selected if you are doing account cycling. If it is not, your period strategies will never rotate on.
  5. Still nothing enabling? On the manager dashboard, click the Enable Strategies button (do not touch your mouse or move anything else) and watch that it turns on all of your strategies. If your connections are not enabled yet, click the connect / enable-connections button first, then click Enable Strategies again.
  6. Still not enabling after that? Close the Ninja Manager and work checks 1 to 8 below by hand. If everything trades fine manually, the manager itself is the issue: take a screenshot of its log and post it in your resource channel so the team can forward it to the dev team.

If a setting looks wrong and you are not sure how to fix it, redo the setup with the Ninja Manager setup guide. There is also a full root-cause card for the manager in the list further down this page.

0 of 8 checks complete

Eight ordered checks. Do them in order; most cases are solved by check 1 or 2. Your progress is saved on this device.

  1. 1

    Confirm NinjaTrader is open and connected

    Nothing fires if NinjaTrader is closed or the account is not logged in. Most no-trades tickets come back to the connection layer, not the strategy.

    1. Confirm NinjaTrader is actually running on the VPS.
    2. Confirm the account is connected (green connection indicator).
    3. If you are stuck in a NinjaTrader login CAPTCHA loop, that is a temporary lockout from too many attempts. Wait about one hour, then retry with no extra spaces or caps lock.
  2. 2

    Confirm the chart is moving live

    A strategy attached to a frozen or delayed chart looks connected but cannot act. Live data is the second layer.

    1. Watch the chart and confirm the price is moving in real time.
    2. Check for a delayed-data label. If you see one, real-time data is not fully active.
    3. A frozen or stale chart usually clears with a fresh NinjaTrader restart.
  3. 3

    Confirm the strategy shows enabled / green / true / synced

    The strategy has to be enabled and reporting healthy before it can take a position. This is the green/true layer.

    1. Open the Strategies tab.
    2. Confirm the strategy shows enabled, green, true, and synced.
    3. Enable strategies one at a time so you can see exactly which one does not turn green.
  4. 4

    Confirm the correct instrument and current contract month

    1. Match each algo to its market and timeframe using the algo cheat sheet shown below this check.
    2. Confirm the contract month is current, not an expired month.
    3. When NinjaTrader prompts you to roll contracts forward, always select Yes, then restart.

  5. 5

    Confirm the correct account is selected in the Strategies tab

    A strategy pointed at an old, blown, or wrong account will not trade the account you expect. Reset or replaced accounts must be reassigned.

    1. In the Strategies tab, confirm each strategy is assigned to the correct, active account.
    2. If you do not see the account dropdown, keep scrolling down inside the strategy settings.
    3. If an account was reset or replaced, it appears in the Accounts tab but must still be assigned to the strategy manually.
  6. 6

    Confirm the updater was run and strategies recreated

    Not recreating strategies after an updater leaves you on old components. Only some strategies turning green is the classic sign. This applies to both prop and cash accounts.

    1. Run the current updater and restart NinjaTrader after it finishes.
    2. Recreate the affected strategies so they use the latest components.
    3. Enable them one at a time, confirming account connected, chart moving, correct account, and green/true for each.
  7. 7

    Confirm the license key is synced

    If several algos have not traded for multiple days, the license key may need a reset.

    1. Log into your whop, where your license key lives.
    2. Click the three dots menu next to your key.
    3. Click Reset, then reconnect in NinjaTrader.
    4. Confirm the strategies reconnect and show enabled/true/green.
  8. 8

    Confirm NinjaTrader was restarted recently, then consider variance

    A stale NinjaTrader session is itself a strategies-not-running cause. Reboot NinjaTrader every 3 days or sooner. Only after every layer above checks out is no trade likely to be normal variance.

    1. Restart NinjaTrader fresh. The rule is to reboot it every 3 days or sooner.
    2. Close and reopen NinjaTrader daily as part of your routine.
    3. If connection, data, green/true, contract, account, updater, and license all check out, then no trade for a session can simply mean the entry conditions did not align.

  9. All eight checks complete

    Nice work. If one of these checks led to a fix, like a license key reset, running the updater, or restarting NinjaTrader, give it one more trading day. Some fixes only show results when the algos get their next entry signals. If a full day passes and you are still not seeing trades, post in your Vincere Discord resource channel: “I went through the no-trades checklist yesterday and I’m still not able to trade.” The team will take it from there.

Common root causes

Every no-trades case traces back to one of these. Open the one that matches your symptom. Most point straight back to a check above; a few add detail for the deeper cases.

1. NinjaTrader not connected, or stuck in a CAPTCHA login loop

Symptom. NinjaTrader is closed, the account is not logged in, or login keeps looping on a CAPTCHA. A CAPTCHA loop is a temporary lockout from too many login attempts.

  1. Start with check 1 above: confirm NinjaTrader is running and the account shows a green connection.
  2. If you hit a CAPTCHA loop, wait about one hour, then retry with no extra spaces and no caps lock.
  3. If a prop firm login also fails, verify the Tradovate credentials directly in the firm portal.

Still stuck? If you have completed checks 1 through 8 of this checklist and it is still happening, post in your resource channel and say you have run the full checklist, describing what you saw at each step so the team can jump straight to the fix.

2. Delayed or frozen chart data

Symptom. The strategy looks attached but the chart is not moving live, or shows a delayed-data label. A strategy on frozen or delayed data cannot act.

  1. Work check 2 above first: confirm the chart is moving and look for a delayed-data label.
  2. Restart NinjaTrader to clear a frozen or stale chart.
  3. If the delay persists on a prop account, see the prop firm delayed-data cause below.

Still stuck? If you have completed checks 1 through 8 of this checklist and it is still happening, post in your resource channel and say you have run the full checklist, describing what you saw at each step so the team can jump straight to the fix.

3. Strategy not enabled, or not showing green / true / synced

See check 3 above. Open the Strategies tab, enable strategies one at a time, and confirm each shows enabled, green, true, and synced.

4. Wrong or expired contract month

See check 4 above, including the algo cheat sheet for the correct market and timeframe. A strategy on an old contract month looks connected but cannot behave correctly.

5. Wrong account selected, or a reset account not reassigned

See check 5 above. Confirm each strategy is assigned to the correct, active account in the Strategies tab; a reset or replaced account must be assigned to the strategy manually.

6. Strategies need to be recreated after an updater

Symptom. After an updater, strategies may need to be recreated so they use the newest components. A common sign is only about half of them turning green. This applies to both prop and cash.

  1. Restart NinjaTrader, then remove and recreate each affected strategy with the updated version.
  2. Enable them one at a time, confirming account connected, chart moving, correct account, and green/true for each.

Still stuck? If one specific strategy still will not turn green, post in your channel and describe which strategy it is and exactly what its row shows in the Strategies tab (enabled, connection, account, sync).

7. License key not syncing

Symptom. If you have worked every check above and the algos still are not trading, the license key itself may need a deeper fix than the reset in check 7.

  1. Log into your whop.
  2. Reshuffle the key. A reshuffle generates a new key, which is different from the reset in check 7.
  3. Run the updater again, then reconnect in NinjaTrader and confirm the strategies show enabled/true/green.

Still stuck? If you have completed checks 1 through 8 of this checklist and it is still happening, post in your resource channel and say you have run the full checklist, describing what you saw at each step so the team can jump straight to the fix.

8. Prop firm delayed data (Tradovate non-professional agreement)

Symptom. Prop firm specific. Strategies can be assigned and green/true but still not receive live data cleanly because the non-professional data agreement is not signed, or one prop connection is affecting the data.

  1. Disconnect all prop connections, then reconnect one firm at a time and watch when the delayed-data label appears.
  2. Log into that firm portal, find the Tradovate credentials, then sign into https://trader.tradovate.com.
  3. Complete the profile and the non-professional trader agreement.
  4. Reconnect the firm in NinjaTrader and confirm the chart is moving live.

Still stuck? If you have completed checks 1 through 8 of this checklist and it is still happening, post in your resource channel and say you have run the full checklist, describing what you saw at each step so the team can jump straight to the fix.

9. New algo charts never added manually

A new algo can show in the Strategies list but not in the chart bar, so it has no chart to act on. Add the chart with the + on the chart tab, using the correct market and timeframe from the algo cheat sheet in check 4 above, then attach the strategy to the correct account.

10. NinjaTrader not restarted recently

See check 8 above. A stale NinjaTrader session is itself a strategies-not-running cause. Reboot NinjaTrader every 3 days or sooner, and close and reopen it daily.

11. No valid setup for the session (normal variance)

Symptom. A lot of price action does not always mean the algo got a valid setup. Only after every check above is healthy is no trade likely to be normal.

  1. Confirm checks 1 through 8 were all healthy when the trades were missed.
  2. If they were, the entry conditions simply may not have aligned that session.
  3. For repeated blown accounts or payout-timing concerns, use the optimizer and gameplanning process rather than adjusting stacks by guesswork.

Still stuck? If you have completed checks 1 through 8 of this checklist and it is still happening, post in your resource channel and say you have run the full checklist, describing what you saw at each step so the team can jump straight to the fix.

12. Ninja Manager did not launch, did not enable strategies, or faulted

Symptom. You rely on the Vincere Ninja Manager for the morning startup and it did not open, did not enable every account or strategy, did not remember your NinjaTrader password, or stopped partway through its sequence.

  1. Work through the Step 0 box at the top of this page: manager running, today's log shows the full sequence, Morning Startup Time set to 6:00 AM or later in Central (VPS) time, Connect on Startup ticked for every live account, and cycling mode matching your account type (prop firm accounts cycle by period; cash accounts use enable all strategies).
  2. Keep your hands off while the manager runs its sequence. Moving the mouse or clicking around can interrupt it. Let it finish, then check the log.
  3. If NinjaTrader did not remember your password, log into NinjaTrader once by hand and make sure remember password is checked.
  4. If an updater run reports the exe is still running, close NinjaTrader and the Ninja Manager, end VincereNinjaManager.exe in Task Manager, restart the VPS, then run the updater again as administrator.
  5. If the manager keeps missing, close it and run the morning routine by hand using checks 1 through 8 until it is resolved. The manager is an early version, so a miss is not something you did wrong. Each morning, verify yourself that NinjaTrader is open, accounts are connected and green, charts are moving live, and each strategy shows enabled, true, and green.

NinjaTrader CAPTCHA lockout

Stuck in a login CAPTCHA loop?

A NinjaTrader CAPTCHA loop is a temporary lockout triggered by too many login attempts. It is not a broken account.

  • Wait about one hour before trying again.
  • On the retry, make sure there are no extra spaces, no caps lock, and no saved-password conflict.
  • If a prop firm login is also failing, confirm the Tradovate credentials directly in the firm portal, and reset the password from the firm side if needed, before reconnecting in NinjaTrader.
