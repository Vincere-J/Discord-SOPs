How PLPI trades

Looks forAn opening range breakout on the Platinum (PL). PLPI marks the high and low of price from 9:00 AM to 9:35 AM ET, then trades a break of that range, long or short.
When it trades9:35 AM to 10:30 AM ET, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Range 9:00 AM to 9:35 AM ET. At most 1 trade a day.
Stop and targetsStop 6 points from entry, one target 12 points away. A trailing stop starts after 10.5 points of profit and follows 10 points behind. Any trade still open closes at 4:30 PM ET.
Contracts and whyAlways 1 contract. Planned risk about $300.
In the backtestBacktest period 10/1/2026 to 10/23/2026: 14 trades, 5 winners and 9 losers, net +$310.00 after fees.
Patterns in this backtest14 trades on 14 trading days, never more than 1 trade a day. 7 long and 7 short. Entries from 9:40 AM to 10:31 AM ET. Of the 14 trades: 5 reached PT1; 9 were full stop losses. Average winner +$602.00, average loser -$300.00. Time in a trade: median 35 minutes, shortest 3 minutes, longest 96 minutes.

PLPI was also walked through in the Backtesting Lab session of 4/18/2026.

Quick terms reminder

BacktestA test of an algo's rules on past market data. These examples come from tick data backtests in the NinjaTrader Strategy Analyzer, not live trading, and results include trading fees.
Long or shortLong means buying to profit if price rises. Short means selling first to profit if price falls.
ContractOne unit of a futures market. PLPI trades the Platinum (PL).
Point and tickA point (pts) is one full unit of price movement. A tick is the smallest step price can move.
SetupThe market pattern an algo waits for before it is allowed to enter.
EntryThe moment and price where the algo opens the trade.
StopThe price where a losing trade is closed automatically. It is set before the trade is entered, so the loss is planned to be about a set size. Fees, and fills a little past the stop price, can make it a little larger.
Target (PT)A price where profit is taken. With more than one contract, part of the position exits at each target (PT1, PT2, PT3).
JAN27The January 2027 futures contract, the one these backtests traded.
Low Risk V1The Low Risk version 1 settings of each algo, the settings used for these backtests.
Trailing stopA stop that follows a winning trade, so it locks in profit if price turns back.
Edge LeverageA setting that makes the next trade after a stop out that day use more contracts. It is off in PLPI Low Risk V1, so the contract count never changes.
CandleOne bar on the chart that covers a set amount of time, like 5 minutes. The thick body shows where price opened and closed in that time, and the thin lines above and below show the highest and lowest prices it reached.
Candle close vs touchPLPI enters the moment price moves through its range level. It does not wait for a candle to close.
Reading the chartsGreen candles closed higher than they opened, red candles closed lower. The blue arrow is the entry, a black X is an exit, the red dashed line is the stop and the green dashed lines are targets. An amber shaded box marks the setup on most charts. Times are Eastern, and each candle is labeled by the time it closed, the same as NinjaTrader.
